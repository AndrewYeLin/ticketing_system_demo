# Synthetic Supporting Documents Guide

> **Academic demo only.** This document is synthetic COMP6708 material and is **not** official PolyU records or finance guidance.

## General demo rule

Only upload documents that are relevant to the synthetic request. The prototype accepts common document formats such as PDF, common image formats, Office documents, CSV, text, MSG, and EML, subject to the application's technical limits.

## Example document purposes

The demo may use:

- receipts for reimbursement lines;
- quotes for procurement;
- approval emails;
- invoices;
- supplier evidence; and
- other supporting documents.

## Safety

Do not upload real bank details, real supplier secrets, real student records, confidential university material, or other sensitive operational data to this course demonstration.

## Missing evidence

If evidence is missing in a demo scenario, describe that fact in the request rather than generating or falsifying a document.
