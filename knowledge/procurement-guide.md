# Synthetic Procurement Guide

> **Academic demo only.** This document is synthetic COMP6708 material and is **not** an official PolyU purchasing policy.

## Demo procurement request

A procurement request in this prototype records a business description, supplier information when available, cost/activity coding, approval information, and supporting documents.

## Demo documents

Depending on the scenario, the prototype may use example documents such as:

- a quote;
- an approval email;
- an invoice after a purchase-order stage; or
- other supporting documents clearly related to the request.

## Demo workflow

The prototype may show stages such as submission to Finance, return to the user for clarification, purchase-order issuance, invoice receipt, and closure.

## Limits

The assistant must not state real tender thresholds, delegation limits, preferred-supplier rules, or legal procurement requirements unless those facts are added later from an approved official source.
