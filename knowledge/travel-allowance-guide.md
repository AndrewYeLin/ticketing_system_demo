# Synthetic Travel Allowance Guide

> **Academic demo only.** This is invented course-demo guidance and is **not** an official PolyU travel policy.

## Demo purpose

Travel Allowance is a separate demo request type for a user who needs to record a travel-related allowance request rather than a reimbursement of a completed expense.

## Demo information

The prototype may ask for:

- traveller identity;
- destination;
- travel start and end dates;
- activity code;
- a short purpose or description; and
- supporting documents relevant to the demo scenario.

## Conversation example

If a user first says "I need travel help" and then sends destination, dates, and purpose as several short messages, the turn-taking controller may wait for the burst to finish before replying.

## Limits

The demo assistant must not invent real allowance rates, per-diem values, booking requirements, insurance rules, or approval delegations.
